Privacy Policy for Lord of Dice

Effective date: October 11, 2026
Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how High Tide Pacific ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Lord of Dice mobile game (the "App"), available on Google Play (package com.tide.lordofdice) and, in the future, the Apple App Store.

If you have questions, contact us at hightidepacific.dev@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Lord of Dice does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • Your game progress, coins, settings, and unlocks are stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases (such as Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change that choice at any time.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information stored on your device

The App saves game data locally on your device. This includes progress, coin balance, unlocked dice, settings, daily ad-reward counters, and whether you have purchased Remove Ads. This data is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it.

On-device motion (shake / flick to roll)

If you purchase Remove Ads, the App may use the device accelerometer to detect a flick or shake so you can roll the dice hands-free. Motion readings are processed only on your device to detect the gesture. We do not send accelerometer data to our servers or to advertising partners.

2.2 Information collected by third-party services

We do not run our own servers to collect your personal data. The third-party services built into the App may collect information as described below.

Google AdMob (advertising)
To serve, limit, measure, and help prevent fraud in ads, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Your consent choices

Depending on your consent and where you live, ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized (based on context only). See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)
In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, and U.S. states with applicable privacy laws, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and store your advertising consent choices.

Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (purchases)
If you buy Remove Ads, the store processes your payment. The App receives a purchase token or receipt to confirm and restore your purchase. Payment details are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, microphone or camera data, health data, or accelerometer / motion readings off the device. The App does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

3. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Run the game and save your progress on your device
  • Detect an on-device flick/shake to roll when Remove Ads is active
  • Show ads, limit how often you see them, and measure their performance (via AdMob)
  • Process, verify, and restore in-app purchases (via Google Play / Apple)
  • Respect and record your ad-consent choices
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some U.S. state laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA), allowing ad partners to use identifiers for personalized advertising may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising." You can opt out as described in Section 5.

4. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent for personalized advertising and related use of device identifiers
  • Legitimate interests for non-personalized ads, fraud prevention, and running the App
  • Performance of a contract for processing your in-app purchases

5. Your choices and rights

Ad consent and personalization

  • You can change your ad-consent choice in the App's settings, where available, or through the consent form shown at launch in regions where it is required.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording varies by device).
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Your privacy rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or "sharing." Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most data can be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. For data held by Google, use Google's tools at https://myaccount.google.com. To make a request to us, email hightidepacific.dev@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Data retention

  • On-device game data stays until you delete the App or clear its data.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob, Google Play, or Apple is kept according to their own retention policies.

7. Children's privacy

Lord of Dice is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at hightidepacific.dev@gmail.com and we will help remove it.

8. Data security

We rely on the security measures of your device's operating system and of Google and Apple. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. International transfers

Third-party providers such as Google may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this URL and change the "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be noted in the App.
